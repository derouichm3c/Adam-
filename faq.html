Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about whitening, sensitivity, shipping and our guarantee. Still stuck? Contact us.

How fast will I see results?

Most people notice a visibly brighter smile in as little as 7 days with the whitening strips, using them just 30 minutes a day. Results vary with your starting shade and habits like coffee, tea and wine.

Will it hurt or make my teeth sensitive?

Our strips are peroxide-free and designed to whiten with dramatically less of the zing and sensitivity that traditional strips cause. The toothpaste is a gentle, low-abrasion formula that won't strip your enamel.

Is LumySmile safe for enamel and gums?

Yes. Everything we make is enamel-safe and gum-friendly — it brightens without damaging or weakening your teeth. If you have specific dental concerns, we always recommend checking with your dentist first.

Are your products natural and eco-friendly?

They're built around natural ingredients like activated charcoal and coconut oil, free from artificial dyes and harsh additives. Our bamboo toothbrushes use a biodegradable handle and BPA-free bristles.

How long does whitening last?

With regular maintenance — brushing with the charcoal toothpaste and occasional strip touch-ups — your brighter smile can last for months. Diet plays a big role, so lighter on the coffee and red wine helps.

Where do you ship and how long does it take?

We offer fast tracked shipping across the US & Canada, free on orders over $50. Orders are processed within 1–3 business days and typically arrive within 3–7 business days, with tracking emailed to you.

What's your return policy?

Every order is backed by our 30-day money-back satisfaction guarantee. If you're not happy, contact us within 30 days of delivery and we'll make it right.

Still have a question?

Our friendly team is here to help and usually replies within 24 hours.
